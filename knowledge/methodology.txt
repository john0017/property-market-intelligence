TOPIC: Analytical methodology
SOURCE: Internal prototype methodology

CONTENT:

The Property Market Intelligence prototype separates evidence into three categories.

1. SOURCE FACTS

Numerical observations obtained from external statistical sources such as Eurostat and the European Central Bank.

2. CALCULATED INDICATORS

Indicators calculated deterministically in Python.

Price / Rent Divergence =
House Price YoY Growth - Rent YoY Growth

Real House Price Growth =
House Price YoY Growth - Inflation

3. AI INTERPRETATION

The language model interprets supplied evidence but is not responsible for calculating the underlying indicators.

The application is designed for market screening and research rather than property-level valuation or investment advice.

Where the supplied evidence is insufficient to answer a question, the AI should explicitly identify the information gap rather than inventing information.
